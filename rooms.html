Included Food Services & Stay Benefits

At HavenNest Lodge, affordable doesn't mean cutting corners on comfort. Every single room booking includes these thoughtful hospitality perks.

Extra Pillows & Blankets

Free On Request

Prefer extra neck support or feeling chilly at night? Extra sanitized microfiber pillows, fleece blankets, or heavy winter quilts are brought to your door free of charge anytime.

Morning Tea & Filter Coffee

Delivered To Room

Wake up refreshed with freshly prepared ginger tea or authentic South Indian filter coffee brought directly to your room between 6:30 AM and 9:30 AM to start your travel day right.

Hot Homestyle Breakfast

$3 / ₹150 Add-on

Enjoy freshly prepared regional delicacies like hot Idli-Vada-Sambar, Poha, Aloo Parathas, or classic Eggs & Toast. Prepared fresh every morning with clean ingredients.

Outside Food Welcome

Free Cutlery & Warming

Ordering through Swiggy, Zomato, or your favorite local street vendor? We provide sanitized dining plates, bowls, spoons, and complimentary microwave reheating in our lounge.

Sealed Water & 24/7 RO

Unlimited Refills

Complimentary sealed packaged mineral water bottle provided in your room, plus unlimited pure chilled and normal UV+RO water dispensing stations located on every floor.

Sanitized Hygiene & Iron Kit

Free On Request

Complimentary dental kit (toothbrush & paste), fresh coconut soap, clean bath towels, and an electric steam iron with fold-out ironing board delivered on request for crisp attire.

Long Stay Discount (7+ Days)

Planning an extended stay for business, exams, or medical transit? Enjoy a flat 10% discount on bookings of 7 consecutive nights or more.

Group & Pilgrimage Bookings

Booking 3 or more rooms together? Get complimentary morning tea, coordinated adjacent floor allotment, and prioritized late check-out till 1:00 PM.

GST & Tax Transparency

Rooms below ₹1,000 / $15 have 0% GST. For rooms between ₹1,000 and ₹7,500, a standard 12% GST applies. We issue valid business tax invoices for expense claims.

Weekend rates apply only for Friday night and Saturday night stays. Weekday rates apply from Sunday night through Thursday night.
Early check-in between 08:00 AM and 11:00 AM is complimentary subject to room vacancy. For arrivals before 08:00 AM, a nominal half-day charge applies. Late check-out up to 01:00 PM is free upon prior request if the room is not pre-assigned.
Absolutely none. We do not ask for security deposits (except for dormitory padlocks if rented for $1 / ₹50 refundable). Wi-Fi, electricity, parking, and hot water are 100% included in the displayed tariff.

Outside food ordering via delivery apps is welcome, with complimentary dining plates, bowls, cutlery, and microwave warming provided in our dining area.
