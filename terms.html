Stay Policies & Terms

Clear, transparent guidelines regarding room reservations, cancellation, ID verification, and guest privacy.

Home / Stay Policies

1. Booking Enquiry & Reservation Confirmation

HavenNest Lodge operates an open booking enquiry system designed to eliminate unnecessary third-party commissions. When you submit a booking enquiry through our website, your chosen room type is temporarily blocked for you.

Within 15 minutes of receiving your enquiry, our front desk team will contact you via phone or WhatsApp to reconfirm your estimated arrival time. Your room is fully confirmed once this verbal or text confirmation is complete.

2. Check-In & Check-Out Hours

  • Standard Check-In: 12:00 PM (Noon)
  • Standard Check-Out: 11:00 AM
  • Early Check-In: Available from 08:00 AM onwards free of charge, subject to room availability upon arrival. For guaranteed check-in prior to 08:00 AM, previous night booking or half-day rate is applicable.
  • Complimentary Cloakroom: Guests arriving before check-in or departing late after check-out are welcome to leave luggage in our secure cloakroom without charge.

3. Mandatory Government Identity Verification

As mandated by municipal police and lodging statutory guidelines, all adult guests (18 years and above) must present an original, valid government-approved photo ID containing address proof at check-in.

Acceptable identification documents:

  • Aadhaar Card (India)
  • Original Passport (Mandatory for all foreign national travelers with valid Visa)
  • Voter ID Card
  • Driver’s License

*Note: PAN Cards and digital phone screenshots without original physical card are not accepted under statutory lodging compliance.

4. Free Cancellation & Date Modifications

We believe in honest, fair hospitality without cancellation penalties. If your transit schedule changes:

  • You may modify your travel dates or cancel your reservation completely at zero penalty up to 24 hours prior to your scheduled check-in date.
  • Simply call or WhatsApp our front desk at +1 (800) 456-7890 or +91 98765 43210 quoting your enquiry reference number.

5. House Rules & Peace of the Property

  • Non-Smoking Policy: All bedrooms, bathrooms, elevators, and interior corridors are strictly smoke-free zones. Smoking is only allowed in designated outdoor zones.
  • Silence Hours: Quiet hours are enforced between 10:30 PM and 06:00 AM. Loud music, shouting, or disturbances in guest corridors will result in immediate management intervention.
  • Visitors: Outside non-staying guests are permitted in the lobby lounge until 09:00 PM. No non-registered visitors are permitted in guest bedrooms after 09:00 PM.

6. Privacy Policy & Guest Data Protection

We respect your privacy. Any personal information (name, telephone number, email address, ID copy) collected during your enquiry or stay is used strictly for reservation management, front-desk communication, and legal police guest registers.

We do not sell, rent, or distribute guest contact details to any third-party marketing companies, insurance agents, or advertisers.
