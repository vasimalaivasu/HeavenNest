Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent stay policies so you know exactly what to expect before booking.

Standard check-in time is 12:00 PM (Noon) and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Early check-in from 08:00 AM onwards is subject to room availability upon arrival. If your train or bus arrives very early, you can safely store your luggage in our cloakroom for free and relax in our waiting lounge.
As per local hospitality regulations, all adult guests must present an original valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driver's License) during check-in. PAN cards are not accepted as valid address proof.
You can conveniently pay at check-in using Cash, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Debit Cards, or Credit Cards. We do not demand advance card details during the enquiry stage.
Yes! We provide on-premises gated parking for both cars and two-wheelers. Parking is 100% complimentary for all registered guests and monitored under 24/7 CCTV surveillance.
We offer a completely flexible cancellation policy. Since no advance booking fee is locked, you can cancel or modify your dates by simply giving our reception a quick call or WhatsApp message at least 24 hours prior to scheduled check-in.
Children under 6 years of age stay free of charge sharing existing bedding with parents. For additional adult guests, extra rollaway mattresses with fresh linen, blanket, and pillow can be provided for a nominal charge ($10 / ₹600 per night).
Yes, absolutely! Extra pillows (soft microfiber or firm contour) and warm fleece blankets or quilts are provided completely free of charge upon calling housekeeping. Fresh morning filter coffee or tea is delivered to your room between 6:30 AM and 9:30 AM. Hot freshly prepared regional breakfast is available as an affordable add-on ($3 / ₹150), and outside food from Swiggy or Zomato is 100% welcome with complimentary dining plates, cutlery, and microwave heating support.
