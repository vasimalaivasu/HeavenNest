Good To Know

House Rules & Guest Guidelines

Simple guidelines that keep HavenNest peaceful, clean, and family-friendly for everyone.

Timings & Check-In

  • Check-in time is from 12:00 PM onwards. Early check-in from 8:00 AM subject to room readiness.
  • Check-out time is strictly 11:00 AM to allow thorough housekeeping for incoming guests.
  • Valid government ID mandatory for each adult occupant upon check-in.

Safety & Decorum

  • Strictly non-smoking property in all bedrooms, hallways, and elevators.
  • Quiet hours observed between 10:30 PM and 06:00 AM for undisturbed guest sleep.
  • Non-staying visitors are permitted in the lobby area until 09:00 PM only.
